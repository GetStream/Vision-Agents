1. **T51: Meta's verify token.** Meta asks the URL owner to echo `hub.challenge` when `hub.verify_token` is «the string you set» (Meta webhooks page). The provider app record has no field for it (AI-906 stores `provider_app_id` and `signing_secret` only). Recommended: accept the provider app id (already in the URL) as the verify token; store nothing. Reason: every delivery is still verified by `X-Hub-Signature-256` under the customer's App Secret, so a foreign app pointed at the URL gets 401s; the handshake echoes digits only, as text/plain with nosniff. Alternative: a write-only `verify_token` on the oauth-client PUT, sealed in a side table (slot 20261011100000).

The author cites `slack_sdk` / `@slack/web-api` pages for `base_url` and headers or marks it `unverified`.

## Lessons (3d)
