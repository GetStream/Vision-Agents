---
description: Distinguish targeted abuse from disagreement, quotation, and community banter.
---
Check who the content addresses and what its context establishes: the conversation around a
message, or the post a comment answers. Weigh threats, intimidation, degrading personal remarks
and repeated unwanted contact. Strong criticism of a market, product, idea or institution is not
automatically an attack on a person. Do not accept an author's claim of joking as proof that the
other person consented. Judge the current content independently of prior flags or punishments.
