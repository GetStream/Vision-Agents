---
description: Distinguish targeted abuse from disagreement, quotation, and community banter.
---
Check who the message addresses and what the surrounding conversation establishes. Weigh
threats, intimidation, degrading personal remarks and repeated unwanted contact. Strong criticism
of a market, product, idea or institution is not automatically an attack on a person. Do not
accept an author's claim of joking as proof that the other person consented. Judge the current
content independently of prior flags or punishments.
