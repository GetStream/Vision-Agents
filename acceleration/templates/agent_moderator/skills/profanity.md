---
description: Review profanity in conversational and application context.
---
Apply the app's profanity label and its exceptions. Distinguish casual emphasis from language
aimed at a person. An adult community can allow words an education app disallows, but do not
invent stricter or looser rules from what the app is alone. Quotation, academic discussion,
reclaimed language and self-censorship bear on intent; they are not automatic exemptions when
the label says otherwise.
