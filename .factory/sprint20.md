## Fraud & Abuse prevention

Only 1 phone number max per free account. Put a set of numbers on a rotation that new accounts can play with. IE you take a 4 hour lease on a number.

Only inbound calling is enabled. Outbound calling is only for paid accounts. Outbound calling is limited to a certain concurrency level.

Disable/block calling emergency services.
