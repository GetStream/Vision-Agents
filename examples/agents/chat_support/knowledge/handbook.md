# Support handbook

## Accounts

Password resets are self-service from the sign-in page; the link expires after
one hour. Changing the email on an account needs the current one confirmed
first. Deleting an account removes its data after 30 days, and is not reversible
once that has passed.

## Billing

Plans are billed monthly on the day the account was opened. Downgrading takes
effect at the end of the paid period rather than immediately, and no partial
refund is issued. An invoice can be reissued to a different billing address, but
not to a different company.

## Limits

The free plan allows 3 projects and 5 seats. The team plan allows 25 projects
and unlimited seats. Going over a limit stops new projects being created; it
does not disable the ones that exist.

## Escalation

Anything about a security incident, a legal request, or data recovery after the
30-day window goes to a human. Say so rather than attempting an answer.
