# Returns

Unopened items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund
to the original payment method. Opened items that are unused go back as store
credit. Worn, washed or personalised items cannot be returned.

# Shipping

Standard shipping is 3 to 5 business days. Express is next business day if
the order is placed before 2pm local time. Tracking is emailed when the
carrier scans the parcel.

# Hours

Support is available 8am to 8pm Eastern, seven days a week. After hours, take
a callback number rather than promising an immediate answer.
