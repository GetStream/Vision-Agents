# Voice agent engineer

We are hiring a voice agent engineer. The role is remote, full time, and
reports to the agents team.

## What the job is

Build and ship voice agents that answer phones and hold conversations. Python
and Go. The stack is Vision Agents, Stream, and the acceleration backend.

## What we look for

Three years building production services. Comfortable with realtime audio,
WebRTC or SIP, and an LLM API. Kind on a call, precise in writing.

## Compensation

$160k to $210k plus equity, depending on experience. Start date is flexible
within a month.
