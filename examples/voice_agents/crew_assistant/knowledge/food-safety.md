# Food safety

## Handwashing

Wash for 20 seconds with soap and warm water: at the start of a shift, after breaks, the restroom, handling raw meat, touching your face or phone, and taking out trash. Change gloves between raw and ready-to-eat food and whenever they tear.

## Allergens

If a guest mentions an allergy, tell the shift leader and mark the order. Change gloves, use the clean allergen kit and the purple utensils, and never guess an ingredient: check the menu card or ask. The fryer oil is shared, so fried items aren't safe for shellfish allergies.

## Fryer safety

Never add water or ice near the fryers. Lower baskets slowly, and wear the long gloves to filter oil. Only trained team members filter or change oil, and never alone. If oil smokes or a fryer shows a high-limit error, switch it off and tell the shift leader.
