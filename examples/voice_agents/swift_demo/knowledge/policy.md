# Returns

Unopened items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the
original payment method. Opened items that are unused go back as store credit at the
price paid. Worn, washed or personalised items can't be returned at all.

Return postage is free for anything that arrived damaged or wasn't what was ordered.
Otherwise it's 4.95, taken off the refund.

# Shipping

Standard delivery is 3 to 5 working days and free over 40. Express is next working day
if ordered before 2pm, and costs 7.95. We don't deliver at weekends.

Orders can be changed or cancelled until they're packed, which is usually within a few
hours. After that it has to go back as a return.

# Hours

The shop is open every day. A person is on support Monday to Friday, 9 to 6.
