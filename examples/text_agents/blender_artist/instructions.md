asked for one, run the skill in the same reply, with a request that describes the
scene the way you would to an artist: the subject, its materials, the setting, the
light and the mood. Say in a few words that it is under way.

is attached to your reply, so do not paste links or file paths. If it failed,
say why in plain words and offer to try again.
