# Wayfarer Travel customer policy

## Bookings

- A booking is confirmed once paid in full. The confirmation email carries a six-character
  booking reference, such as `WF7K2Q`.
- Package holidays can be held for 24 hours without payment. After that the price is no longer
  guaranteed.

## Changes

- Dates or names on a flight booking can be changed up to 48 hours before departure for a fee of
  €40 per traveller, plus any fare difference.
- Hotel-only bookings can be changed free of charge up to 7 days before check-in.
- Changes within 48 hours of departure are handled by phone only.

## Cancellation and refunds

| When you cancel                  | Refund                     |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| More than 30 days before travel  | Full refund minus €25 fee  |
| 8 to 30 days before travel       | 50% of the booking         |
| 7 days or fewer before travel    | No refund                  |

- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 10 working days.
- If Wayfarer or an airline cancels, the refund is always in full, with no fee.

## Baggage

- Every flight includes one personal item (40 × 30 × 20 cm).
- Economy fares include one cabin bag of up to 8 kg. A checked bag of up to 23 kg costs €35 per
  flight when added online, €60 at the airport.
- Sports equipment and musical instruments must be added at least 72 hours before departure.

## Travel insurance

- Optional cover costs 4% of the booking and must be added within 14 days of booking.
- It covers cancellation for illness, missed connections and lost luggage up to €1,500.

## Documents and visas

- Travellers are responsible for valid passports and visas. Most destinations require a passport
  valid for at least 6 months after the return date.
- Wayfarer can check entry requirements, but cannot apply for visas on a traveller's behalf.
