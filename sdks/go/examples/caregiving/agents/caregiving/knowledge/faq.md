# What care costs

Rates and prices: babysitters, nannies and after-school care cost $22 an hour on average.
Senior care costs $26 an hour on average, and overnight care is a flat $280 a night.
Families pay caregivers directly; booking an interview is free.

# Background checks

Every caregiver listed has passed an identity check and a criminal background check in the
last 12 months. Families can request a driving record check for $15.

# Interviews

Interviews are 30 minutes, by video or in person. Good questions to ask:

- How long have you cared for children or older adults, and in what settings?
- How would you handle a medical emergency?
- Are you comfortable with pets, cooking or light housework?
- Can you share two references we can call?

A family can cancel an interview up to two hours before it starts.
